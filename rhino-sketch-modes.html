        Questions to explore next

        • Saving ink. Should sketches be saved in the .3dm as their own object type (like annotations), or only as a sketch layer that never exports?
        • Symmetry. Mirror ink live across a plane through the 3D cursor while you draw.
        • Marks into Grasshopper. A circled region could become a surface input, an arrow a number slider.
        • Talk while drawing. Words spoken during a stroke attach to the mark drawn at that moment.
        • iPad as a sketch pad. Use Sidecar or an iPad app as a pen surface that draws into the Mac viewport.
        • Client redlines. Clients mark up a shared web view, and their marks come back into Rhino on the same objects.
