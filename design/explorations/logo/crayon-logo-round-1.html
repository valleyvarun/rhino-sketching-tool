Crayon: logo concepts

Six directions. All use graphite ink, warm paper and our cobalt. Nothing orange, no starbursts, nothing that reads as Claude. The large marks carry a real wax-crayon texture for the human feel; the small sizes are clean so they stay crisp in Rhino's panel tab.

Each concept: the textured mark on paper, the lockup with the wordmark on light and dark, app icons on paper, ink and cobalt, the Rhino panel header at 20 px, and a 16 px tab icon.
