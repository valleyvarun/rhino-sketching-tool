Agent panel proposal

Gecko's structure (header mark, Chat and History tabs, a welcome screen, a composer with upload, mode, context and voice, run steps and a done line) in our white Paper Cobalt look. Buttons are neutral greys; blue appears only for focus, switches, counts and the done line. Everything is clickable: try a suggestion, the + button, Mode, Sees and the mic, or drop an image on the composer.

  1. 1
    Mode (was “Sketch → 3D”)

    Says what the agent will do with your sketch: make a 3D model, read a floor plan, act on your numbered marks, or only answer questions. Each option has a one-line explanation, so nobody has to guess.

  2. 2
    Sees (was “Context”)

    What the agent is given: sketches marked AI in Info, the 3D cursor, the current view, selected objects and the active layout. The number shows how many are on. “Preview” shows the exact text sent to Varun's model.

  3. 3
    Upload

    + opens: upload an image or PDF, capture the viewport, or paste a sketch. You can also drop files on the composer or paste with Ctrl+V. Images show as tiles you can remove.

  4. 4
    Voice

    Talk while you sketch. The composer outline turns dark and level bars show it is listening. No glow.

  5. 5
    Results you can keep

    Run steps tick off as the model works, then a result card shows the plan and the 3D massing. Keep adds it to the model and it appears in Info › What changed with its own Undo.

  6. 6
    History

    Past sessions with a thumbnail of what was built, as in Gecko.
