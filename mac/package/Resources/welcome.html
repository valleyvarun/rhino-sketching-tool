Install Crayon for Rhino 8

Draw on your model, pin comments and work with an assistant inside Rhino.

Setup installs Crayon for your account. It looks for Claude Code and Codex and downloads any that are missing. On Apple silicon with Rhino 8.20 or later, it also sets up RhinoMCP so an assistant can work with your model.

Save your work and quit Rhino before continuing. An internet connection is needed to download missing components.
